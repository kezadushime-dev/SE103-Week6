Why committing secrets is dangerous: Hardcoding or committing secrets such as API keys, passwords, or tokens directly into a public or private repository exposes sensitive resources to unauthorized access. Malicious actors use automated bots to scan commit histories for exposed credentials. Once a secret is pushed into a Git commit history, removing it entirely requires rewriting repository history and immediately revoking the compromised credential.
