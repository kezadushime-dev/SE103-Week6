Why smaller commits are better than one giant commit:
Smaller commits make it easier to understand what changed in a project. They help developers find and fix problems because each commit focuses on a specific purpose. They also make it easier to review work and understand the history of a project. If something goes wrong, a small commit is easier to investigate or undo.
